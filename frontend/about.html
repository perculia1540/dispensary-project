About MUST Dispensary

Providing accessible and quality healthcare services to the MUST community.

Who We Are

MUST Dispensary is a healthcare facility established to provide essential medical services to students, staff and members of the surrounding community.

The dispensary provides patients with access to healthcare services in a safe, professional and supportive environment.

Our Goal

To promote good health and provide reliable healthcare services to the MUST community.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide quality, accessible and patient-centered healthcare services while promoting health and wellbeing.

Our Vision

To become a trusted healthcare facility that contributes to a healthy and productive university community.

Our Values

Professionalism, confidentiality, respect, compassion and commitment to quality healthcare.
